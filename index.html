FAA Delay Definition

A flight is labeled DELAYED = 1 if departure delay > 15 minutes OR if the flight was cancelled. All other flights are DELAYED = 0. This matches the official FAA on-time performance standard.

About Logistic Regression Coefficients

Instead of a feature importance chart, Logistic Regression uses signed coefficients. A positive coefficient means the feature increases delay probability; a negative one decreases it. Key positive drivers include late-night hours and high precipitation.

What We Learned

Key Insights

Time of Day Is the Top Predictor

Scheduled departure hour ranked #1 in every model. Flights later in the day inherit cascading delays from earlier aircraft. The importance gap was large: departure hour scored 0.083 in GB permutation importance vs. 0.001 for precipitation.

Weather Had Surprisingly Low Impact

Despite adding 4 weather features, they ranked last in Gradient Boosting permutation importance. Year and month (capturing seasonality and COVID-era disruptions) far outranked precipitation and wind speed as predictors.

No Single Model Wins Everything

Random Forest had the best accuracy (69.9%) and precision (33.8%). Gradient Boosting led on recall (64.9%), F1 (43.3%), and ROC-AUC (0.717). The best choice depends on whether you prioritize catching delays or avoiding false alarms.

Year and Season Drive Predictions

YEAR ranked 2nd and MONTH 3rd in Gradient Boosting importance. This reflects the dramatic shift in flight patterns during COVID-19 (2020-2021) and strong seasonal demand variations across summer, holidays, and winter weather periods.

Class Imbalance Is a Hard Problem

With only ~20% of flights delayed, a naive model that always predicts "on time" would score 80% accuracy. Using class_weight="balanced" forced the models to prioritize recall on the minority class, at the cost of lower precision.

Airline Identity Beats Airport

AIRLINE_CODE ranked 4th in GB importance (0.033), well above ORIGIN (8th, 0.010) and DEST (10th, 0.005). A carrier's systemic on-time performance record is a stronger signal than which airport a flight departs from.
